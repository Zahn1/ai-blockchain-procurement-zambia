Publish a tender

Acting as officer1 (procurement officer, ZPPA). The document is stored on IPFS and its SHA-256 hash is written to the ledger with a timestamp.

Published tenders

From the back end's off-chain records. Status is worked out from the ledger closing time.

Submit a sealed bid

Submitted on your behalf through the gateway ledger identity. The file is encrypted with a new AES-256-GCM key, the ciphertext goes to IPFS, and only its hash is recorded on the ledger. Bids after the closing time are rejected by the chaincode.

You bid for as , your company's code name on the ledger.

Signed receipt

The supplier's proof of on-time submission, signed with the gateway's private key.

Submit a bid to see its receipt.

Search tenders, contracts and payments

Open to everyone, no sign-in. Search by reference, title, company or IFMIS payment reference.

Open data (OCDS)

Download procurement records in the Open Contracting Data Standard, including tender amendments, contract milestones and payments. Bid contents and personal data are not included.

Check a published record against the ledger

Upload a copy of a tender document, signed contract or completion certificate. If even one character has changed, its hash will not match the one on the ledger.

Check a bid receipt

Paste a receipt (JSON). Any change to it breaks the signature.

Ledger record

Read live from the blockchain: the tender, every version of it, and the sealed bids (hashes only).

Bid opening and evaluation

Bids open only after the closing time and once the required committee members have each signed their approval on the ledger. Each bid is checked against its ledger hash when it is opened.

Ready to award

Tenders whose evaluation committee has made its recommendation. Awarding records the evaluation report hash on the ledger.

Contracts

Payment happens when the inspector's certificate passes the ledger's checks and neither the contract nor its tender is flagged as high risk.

Risk alerts

Every alert on the ledger. Record a decision (cleared, monitor or refer) with the reason; the hash of the decision is recorded on the ledger, signed with your ledger identity. An unreviewed or referred alert scoring 70 or more holds payments on its contract.

Procurement history

The complete record of a procurement, from tender publication to final payment, read from the ledger.

Reports

Download each report as a PDF or a spreadsheet (Excel). Figures come from the ledger and the system's records at the moment you download.
