Transparent Procurement Prototype

Hyperledger Fabric (ZPPA + OAG), IPFS, AES-256-GCM

Publish a tender

Acting as officer1 (procurement officer, ZPPA). The document is stored on IPFS and its SHA-256 hash is written to the ledger with a timestamp.

Published tenders

From the back end's off-chain records. Status is worked out from the ledger closing time.

Submit a sealed bid

Submitted through the gateway identity on the supplier's behalf. The file is encrypted with a new AES-256-GCM key, the ciphertext goes to IPFS, and only its hash is recorded on the ledger. Bids after the closing time are rejected by the chaincode.

Signed receipt

The supplier's proof of on-time submission, signed with the gateway's private key.

Submit a bid to see its receipt.

Check a document against the ledger

Upload a copy of a tender document. If even one character has changed, its hash will not match the one on the ledger.

Check a bid receipt

Paste a receipt (JSON). Any change to it breaks the signature.

Ledger record

Read live from the blockchain: the tender, every version of it, and the sealed bids (hashes only).

Prototype for Chapter 3 requirements 3.5.3 and 3.5.4. Sign-in with a one-time code (3.5.1) is not part of this prototype; each page acts as a fixed enrolled user.
