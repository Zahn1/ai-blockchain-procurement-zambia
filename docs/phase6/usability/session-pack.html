Usability evaluation · facilitator's guide · not for participants

Transparent Procurement prototype: usability sessions

Zhaninge Chipeta (202204924), BSc Information Technology, ZCAS University. Chapter 4 evaluation.

Aim

To find out whether people can complete the main tasks of each role without help, and how usable they find the system, measured with the System Usability Scale (SUS). Target: 5–8 participants, about 35 minutes each.

Before each session

  1. Start the system (README, “Running the prototype”) and open http://localhost:5173 in a fresh browser window.
  2. Sign in the four role accounts in four separate browser windows or profiles, so the participant never needs the authenticator app: officer officer@moh.test, bidder grace@alpha.test, regulator regulator@zppa.test, and a private window that is not signed in (public).
  3. Put the files from docs/phase6/usability/files/ on the desktop.
  4. Give the participant number P1, P2 …; their tender reference is ZPPA/MOH/2026/UX-P1 and so on.

During the session

  1. Read the information sheet together and collect the signed consent form (sheet 2).
  2. Give the task sheet (sheet 3). Switch to the right browser window for each task. Ask them to think aloud.
  3. Do not help unless they are stuck for two minutes or ask. Record on the observation sheet (sheet 4): S succeeded unaided, H succeeded with help, F failed or gave up; time in seconds; what went wrong.
  4. After the tasks, the SUS questionnaire (sheet 5) and the three short questions.

Afterwards

Type each participant's results into results.csv (one row each), then run python3 docs/phase6/usability/score.py. It writes results.md with each SUS score, the mean and the task success rates. A SUS score of 68 is average; above 80 is good.

Only real sessions count. Do not fill in example rows: Chapter 4 reports what participants did.
Sheet 2 · participant information and consent

Taking part in a usability test

Transparent Procurement prototype, a final-year project at ZCAS University.

What is this about?

I am testing a prototype system that records government procurement (tenders, bids, contracts and payments) on a shared ledger so it cannot be changed without detection. I want to know whether it is easy to use. I am testing the system, not you. There are no wrong answers.

What will you do?

About eight short tasks, such as publishing a tender or checking a document, while thinking aloud. Then a short questionnaire. It takes about 35 minutes. All data in the system is invented; you will not use any real accounts.

Your information

  • I will note what you do, how long each task takes and what you say. Nothing is recorded on video or audio.
  • Your name is not kept with your answers; you are identified only by a participant number such as P3.
  • Results appear in my dissertation only as numbers and anonymous quotes.
  • You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your answers to be removed until 30 November 2026.

Sheet 3 · your tasks

Tasks

Please think aloud as you work. Tell me when you think you have finished each task.

1 · Procurement officer. Publish a tender for 120 office chairs. Use the reference ZPPA/MOH/2026/UX-P__ (your number), estimated value K45,000, closing in 60 minutes, with the document tender-chairs.txt from the desktop.
2 · Bidder (Alpha Computers). Submit a bid for the chairs tender you just published, using bid-chairs.txt. Keep proof that you submitted it.
3 · Member of the public. Someone has emailed you tender-chairs-altered.txt and says it is the official tender for your chairs tender. Find out whether that is true.
4 · Procurement officer. The supplier on contract AWD-1823F9 says they have not been paid for the second milestone. Find out why.
5 · Bidder (Alpha Computers). Which of your company's bids have been successful?
6 · Regulator. Look at the highest-scoring risk alert that has not been reviewed yet. Decide what should happen and record your decision with a reason.
7 · Regulator. When was the contract for tender ZPPA/MOH/2026/F0001 signed, and what is the IFMIS reference of its first payment?
8 · Member of the public. Which company won tender ZPPA/MOH/2026/F0001, and how much has it been paid so far?
